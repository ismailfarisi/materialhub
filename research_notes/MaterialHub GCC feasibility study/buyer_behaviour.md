# B2B Buyer Purchasing Behaviour in the UAE/GCC (SMEs, printing presses, packaging converters, gifting/perfume/F&B brands), 2026

Method note: about 17 web searches and fetch attempts (Nov 2026). Full-text fetches of atradius.de and adsmehub.ae failed because the session's network allowlist does not include those domains. Most findings below come from search-result summaries of press releases and trade media, not from the primary reports themselves. Verify exact figures against the originals (atradius.ae, Visa newsroom, Ministry of Finance) before quoting them in a final report. Wherever a claim is mine and has no source, it sits under "Inferences" or "Gaps".

## 1. Channels: how buyers discover suppliers and place orders (WhatsApp, phone, walk-in, marketplaces, Instagram, trade shows, digital-buying surveys)

### Takeaway
Messaging, especially WhatsApp, is the dominant expected interface between UAE businesses and their customers, and trade shows in Dubai (Paperworld ME, Gulf Print & Pack) remain big physical discovery venues for paper and packaging. I found no MENA-specific B2B digital-buying survey (McKinsey B2B Pulse has no GCC cut that I could find) and no authoritative WhatsApp Business adoption rate for SMEs.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp preference (consumer-side, but these are the same people who run SMEs). In a Zbooni-commissioned YouGov survey of 1,000 UAE residents (2024 MENA cCommerce Report):
  - 85% would prefer WhatsApp for interacting with companies.
  - 65% had used WhatsApp to contact a business about a product or service in the past year.
  - 84% said WhatsApp is more helpful than email.
  - About 9 in 10 prefer human support over chatbots or generative AI.
  - Sources: [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/85-percent-of-uae-residents-want-businesses-to-use-whatsapp/), [Khaleej Times on human vs chatbot](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-human-or-chatbot-heres-what-90-of-residents-prefer-for-customer-support).
- UAE SME sales channels (Zbooni 2021 SME survey, 200+ SMEs, run by duke+mir): Instagram ranked first for growing online sales, followed by WhatsApp, branded websites and Facebook. 44.2% called WhatsApp essential for online sales — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/uae/en/story/ZAWYA20210824070203/); [mid-east.info](https://mid-east.info/zbooni-reveals-results-of-nationwide-uae-sme-survey-report). The data is old (2021) and the sponsor is a vendor.
- Saudi Arabia: one vendor blog says WhatsApp has 25M+ users with 83% daily usage (citing Statista 2024) and that KSA ranks among the top countries for per-capita usage (citing DataReportal) — [Deewan](https://www.deewan.sa/blog/why-now-is-the-time-to-choose-whatsapp-for-your-business-in-saudi-arabia). This is secondary and vendor-sourced.
- Statista tracks the countries with the most WhatsApp Business app downloads — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1276030/whatsapp-business-downloads-leading-countries). I could not see the GCC figures.
- Physical clusters: directory listings place Dubai print, packaging and paper suppliers in:
  - Al Qusais Industrial Areas 1–3 (for example, packaging firms, printing presses and paper distributors)
  - Deira (Port Saeed, Al Ras)
  - Ras Al Khor
  - Dubai Investment Park
  - Sources: [Yellow Pages UAE: packaging-printing](https://www.yellowpages-uae.com/uae/packaging-printing), [paper distributors](https://www.yellowpages-uae.com/uae/paper-distributors), [printing press](https://www.yellowpages-uae.com/uae/dubai/printing-press), [HiDubai: paper traders in Al Ras / Hor Al Anz / Al Qusais](https://www.hidubai.com/businesses/trade-xpress-paper-products-trading-b2b-services-distributors-wholesalers-al-qusais-2-dubai).
- Paperworld Middle East plus Gifts & Lifestyle ME (DWTC, 11–13 Nov 2025):
  - Exhibitors: reported as 615 from 39 countries in one release and 650+ from 38 countries in another. The 615 figure may refer to 2024.
  - Visitors: about 12,000 projected, and 12,900 per a listing site.
  - Named UAE exhibitors include Farook International, Al Fahidi Stationery, Pan Gulf Marketing and Ittihad Paper Mill.
  - Sources: [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/events-and-conferences/paperworld-middle-east-and-gifts-and-lifestyle-middle-east-conclude-largest-ever-edition-jfc7ztqu), [Zawya 650 brands](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/events-and-conferences/paperworld-middle-east-and-gifts-and-lifestyle-middle-east-unites-over-650-regional-and-international-brands-across-five-halls-at-dubai-world-trade-centre-this-week-mbrofve9), [TradeArabia](https://www.tradearabia.com/News/330250/600-global-brands-to-join-major-paper-and-gifting-events-in-Dubai).
- Gulf Print & Pack 2024 (Dubai): 8,331 visitors from 101 countries and 256 exhibitors — [gulfprintpack.com](https://www.gulfprintpack.com/).
- Global B2B Pulse (McKinsey):
  - Buyers use in-person, remote and digital self-serve channels in roughly equal measure; about 8 in 10 rate omnichannel at least as effective (2021, 3,496 respondents in the Americas, APAC and Europe).
  - The 2026 (tenth) edition, with about 4,000 respondents in 13 countries, calls digital and e-commerce "table stakes".
  - Neither edition has a GCC breakdown.
  - Sources: [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/survey-global-b2b-decision-maker-response-to-covid-19-crisis), [eCommerceNews Asia](https://ecommercenews.asia/story/mckinsey-flags-widening-b2b-gap-as-ai-lifts-leaders).
- In a Tradeling-commissioned PSB Middle East survey (UAE and KSA, pandemic era, exact date unconfirmed), 82% on average said their B2B e-commerce use had increased because of the pandemic — summarised via [adsmehub](https://www.adsmehub.ae/en/explore/post-details/study-reliable-online-payments-crucial-to-b2b-e-commerce-growth-in-the-gcc). Per its title, the study stresses that reliable online payments are crucial to B2B e-commerce growth.

### Inferences
- Discovery and ordering for small trade buyers is probably a mix of WhatsApp (catalogue PDFs, photos, voice notes, price lists), phone, walk-in visits to Deira, Al Qusais and Sharjah clusters, and annual trade-show contact. A marketplace that does not plug into WhatsApp (share links, order-status notifications, quote chat bridged to WhatsApp) would be fighting the default habit.
- Retail and craft buyers and small gifting, perfume and F&B brands look more Instagram-led, in line with the Zbooni SME channel ranking.
- Trade shows are a cheap, dense channel for vendor acquisition: hundreds of paper and packaging exhibitors in one hall each November.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2024–2026 WhatsApp Business adoption rate for UAE or KSA SMEs. Meta, TDRA and Monsha'at sources did not surface.
- No data on Alibaba or IndiaMART usage by UAE or GCC buyers.
- No McKinsey B2B Pulse GCC cut. No Redseer or Dubai Chamber report on B2B digital buying surfaced.
- Big 5 is a construction show and is of marginal relevance. I did not research it.

## 2. Relationships, trust and language communities (wasta, trader loyalty, South Asian-run trading houses)

### Takeaway
I found almost no direct, citable evidence on wasta, trader loyalty or the language make-up of the UAE paper and packaging trade. The best proxy evidence comes from Indian wholesale, where investors and founders say trade happens "within existing circles of trust" and that trust plus credit, not procurement, is the core pain point.

### Cited Findings
- A Lightspeed investor describes Indian trading networks as ones where entities primarily do business within their existing circles of trust. Udaan's co-founder said the pain point "wasn't just procurement — it was trust and credit" — [AVCJ](https://www.avcj.com/avcj/official-record/3008829/deal-focus-lightspeed-boosts-udaan-s-b2b-drive); [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/story/can-udaan-become-the-flipkart-of-b2b-e-commerce-in-india/).
- Kerala-origin entrepreneurs account for some of the biggest business names in the UAE, in retail, education and healthcare. The article does not cover the paper trade — [The National via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uae/the-national-news/20180110/281655370469684).
- About 9 in 10 UAE residents prefer human support over AI, which signals a preference for human contact even in digital channels — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-human-or-chatbot-heres-what-90-of-residents-prefer-for-customer-support).

### Inferences
- Unverified and based on general knowledge, so confirm before using: many Deira, Al Ras and Al Qusais paper and stationery traders appear to be South Asian-run, and the working languages are likely English, Hindi/Urdu and Malayalam more than Arabic. Arabic matters more for Emirati and Saudi buyers and for government or corporate procurement. KSA buyers likely need Arabic-first UX.
- Credit is extended on relationships, so buyers are loyal to traders who carry them on credit. A marketplace cannot easily replicate that unless it underwrites credit itself (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No academic or survey source found on wasta in SME procurement, or on supplier loyalty and switching rates in the GCC.
- No data on the ethnic or language composition of UAE paper and packaging traders.

## 3. Payment behaviour (cheques, credit terms, cash, late payment, cards, cheque law, BNPL)

### Takeaway
Trade credit is the norm in UAE B2B: about half of sales are on credit, with average terms of about 47 days, and a large share is paid late (58% of credit sales in 2025; about 2 in 5 invoices in 2026). Delays worsened in 2026. Cheques remain common but are giving way to digital rails, and bounced cheques for insufficient funds have been a civil, not criminal, matter since January 2022. B2B BNPL and trade-credit fintechs (Comfi, Bynow, Mala) are active.

### Cited Findings
- Atradius Payment Practices Barometer, UAE 2025 (survey in the second half of Q2 2025):
  - About 50% of B2B sales are on credit, with average terms of 47 days.
  - About 58% of credit sales are paid late.
  - Bad debts average about 8% of overdue invoices.
  - In FMCG, terms are about 40 days and 56% of firms expect more insolvencies.
  - Half of UAE firms expect insolvencies to rise.
  - 42% combine internal provisioning with credit insurance.
  - Sources: [TradeArabia](https://www.tradearabia.com/News/297337/Firms-concerned-over-rising-bad-debts-in-UAE-finds-survey), [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/money/mediaoutreach/2025/08/04/atradius-survey-reveals-cautious-optimism-among-uae-businesses-despite-rising-bad-debts-and-increased-geopolitical-uncertainty/399401).
- Atradius UAE 2026 (survey in the second half of Q2 2026, released Aug 2026):
  - Credit sales average 47% of B2B transactions, and nearly half of firms extended more credit.
  - About 2 in 5 invoices are paid late, and more firms report worsening payment behaviour than improving.
  - Causes of delay: customer cash-flow constraints (49%), then banking processes, internal approvals and undelivered goods.
  - Effects: 47% of firms have higher financing needs and 46% less liquidity headroom.
  - Outlook: 46% expect default risk to rise.
  - Sources: [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/technology-telecom/payment-delays-continue-to-strain-uae-businesses-as-trade-credit-expands-427908), [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/mediaoutreach/2026/08/11/payment-delays-continue-to-strain-uae-businesses-as-trade-credit-expands/480069).
- Atradius UAE 2023: 33% of invoices were paid on time (down from 38%), and late payments affected 56% of B2B invoiced sales — reported in [IntelligentFin](https://www.intelligentfin.tech/?p=8732) / [ibsintelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/uae-b2b-credit-risk-increases-due-to-prolonged-payment-delays-research-reveal).
  - Conflict: the 2025 figure (58% of credit sales late) and the 2026 figure (about 40% of invoices late) use different bases. They should not be read as an improvement without checking the full report.
- Historical (Coface, 2016–17): average UAE payment terms were 60–90 days, with average delays of 30–60 days — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/property/late-payments-to-uae-firms-doubled-last-year-says-credit-insurer-coface-1.59990). I found no Coface GCC survey for 2024–2026.
- Cheque law: from 2 Jan 2022 (amendments under Decree-Law 14/2020), a cheque bounced for insufficient funds is no longer a crime unless bad faith is involved. A bounced cheque can be taken directly to an execution judge as an executive instrument; fraud and forgery remain criminal. Sources differ on whether Federal Decree-Law 50/2022 (the new Commercial Transactions Law) is the current governing text — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/banking-in-uae/decriminalising-bounced-cheques-in-uae-all-you-need-to-know), [CBUAE PDF](https://centralbank.ae/media/bjhfhdki/the-central-bank-the-ministry-of-economy-and-the-ministry-of-justice-implement-amendments-to-the-commercial-transactions-law-regarding-decriminalisation-of-cheques-without-funds_en.pdf), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/banking/uae-bounced-cheque-cases-decriminalised--what-happens-to-old-cases-1.1641465578551).
- Visa SME Megatrends (Jul 2024): cheques are "still commonly used in the UAE", but SMEs are moving toward low-cost, faster digital options (Tap to Phone, Visa Direct). Opening an SME bank account is still challenging and expensive — [Visa](https://ae.review.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/prl-22072024.html).
- Visa Value of Acceptance (Mar 2024; 250 nano, micro and small retailers, interviewed Dec 2023): over 70% saw revenue and footfall rise after accepting digital payments — [Visa](https://ae.review.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases/prl-05032024.html). Earlier Visa research found 71% of UAE small businesses are or plan to be cashless, against 59% globally — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/half-of-uae-consumers-plan-to-be-all-digital-on-payments-by-2024---and-smes-are-joining-them-visa-survey-1.1642043616045).
- Marketplace-embedded credit:
  - Tradeling offered direct credit lines with 30, 60 or 90-day terms across 14 categories — [Khaleej Times](https://khaleejtimes.com/business/al-etihad-credit-bureau-tradeling-to-offer-businesses-in-uae-better-credit-facilities?amp=1).
  - It extended AED 15M in terms to 65+ UAE SMEs in H1 2022 — [Zawya](https://zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/tradeling-extended-aed15mln-in-credit-terms-to-over-65-uae-based-smes-during-h1-of-2022-ovxvs9ds?amp=1).
  - It partnered with Al Etihad Credit Bureau (Jun 2022) to set terms from credit scores, and earlier with Invoice Bazaar on revolving credit (2020) — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/news/b2b-payments/2020/online-marketplace-tradeling-offers-revolving-credit-smbs).
- B2B BNPL players:
  - Comfi (Dubai): suppliers can offer up to 90-day terms in real time. It reported a $65M pre-Series A of equity plus debt (date unconfirmed) and earlier a $5M debt facility — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/?p=1969007), [TechMoran](https://techmoran.com/?p=227709).
  - Bynow (Riyadh, founded 2022): Shariah-compliant BNPL for SMEs; $1.2M from Merak Capital — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/fintech/merak-capital-invests-12mln-in-bynow-to-foster-regional-expansion-rje6wapt).
  - Mala (KSA, founded 2024): "procure now pay later" with suppliers paid upfront; $7M pre-seed — [ScenoNoise](https://dev.scenenoise.com/Business/Saudi-B2B-Procure-Now-Pay-Later-Platform-Mala-Secures-7-Million).

### Inferences
- A cards-only or prepaid checkout fits B2C and sample orders but will under-serve repeat trade buyers, who expect 30–60+ day terms. MaterialHub's current payment set (Stripe, Tabby/Tamara, COD) is consumer-BNPL oriented. Repeat B2B volume likely needs invoice or net terms through a B2B BNPL partner (Comfi-type), with the supplier paid upfront, so vendors are not asked to carry credit risk on unknown buyers.
- Because payment delays worsened in 2026, vendors have a reason to accept a platform that guarantees payment. This could be a supply-side selling point.
- Since cheque bounces were decriminalised, PDCs carry less coercive weight. That probably nudges suppliers toward credit insurance or fintech-backed terms. I have no direct evidence on whether PDC usage has fallen.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on PDC usage in B2B trade, or on the share of business purchases made by corporate card versus bank transfer versus cash.
- No KSA or Kuwait payment-practice barometer data found for 2024–2026.

## 4. Decision drivers (price, MOQ, speed, availability, samples, consistency, VAT invoices)

### Takeaway
Direct survey evidence on decision drivers for GCC packaging and print buyers did not surface. Regulatory evidence shows that compliant tax invoices will become machine-mandatory: UAE B2B e-invoicing through Accredited Service Providers starts in phases from 2027.

### Cited Findings
- UAE e-invoicing:
  - Peppol-based 5-corner model through Accredited Service Providers, overseen by the Ministry of Finance.
  - Pilot announced 26 Jun 2026; Ministerial Decision 244/2025 amended 10 May 2026.
  - Firms with over AED 50M revenue must appoint an ASP by about 30 Oct 2026; others by about 31 Mar 2027. B2B go-live is phased from Jan 2027.
  - Penalty for non-compliance: AED 5,000 per month (Cabinet Decision 106/2025).
  - Dates conflict across sources; earlier reports said July 2026 — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2024/10/tnf-uae-implementation-mandatory-e-invoicing-july-2026.html), [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-e-invoicing-mandate-timelines-business-compliance-service-provider-accreditation/), [Fonoa](https://fonoa.com/blog/uae-publishes-details-upcoming-e-invoicing-mandate), [fiscal-requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5746-uae-defines-e-invoicing-scope-and-implementation-timeline-through-two-ministerial-decisions).
- Atradius 2026 lists "undelivered goods/services" among the causes of payment delay. Fulfilment reliability therefore ties directly to cash collection — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/technology-telecom/payment-delays-continue-to-strain-uae-businesses-as-trade-credit-expands-427908).

### Inferences
- For converters and printing presses, the likely drivers in order are: price per unit at their quantity, availability or stock for the exact GSM, size and finish, lead time (often same or next day in Dubai), consistency between batches, then MOQ flexibility. Brands (gifting, perfume, F&B) probably weigh samples, finish quality and MOQ for custom rigid boxes more heavily. All of this is hypothesis and should be validated through interviews.
- VAT invoices showing the buyer's TRN are a hygiene requirement now. From 2027, e-invoice compliance through an ASP becomes a selling point for a marketplace that issues invoices on vendors' behalf, and a burden for small traders, who may welcome outsourcing it.

### Gaps
- No GCC survey ranking B2B purchase criteria for packaging or print materials.
- No data on how much sampling matters or how sensitive buyers are to MOQ.

## 5. Who the users are and how digitally literate they are (owner, procurement, designer; mobile vs desktop)

### Takeaway
No direct data was found on buyer roles or device split for GCC B2B materials buying. Evidence points to mobile- and messaging-first behaviour among UAE residents and small businesses.

### Cited Findings
- 65% of UAE residents contacted a business through WhatsApp in the past year, and 84% find it more helpful than email — [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/85-percent-of-uae-residents-want-businesses-to-use-whatsapp/).
- UAE SMEs rank Instagram and WhatsApp above their own websites for online sales (2021) — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/uae/en/story/ZAWYA20210824070203/).
- Saudi academic work (2011–2017): B2B and e-procurement adoption is driven mainly by organisational IT readiness, management support and regulation. Cultural inertia and business culture are barriers. Customer pressure affects usage more than initial adoption — [Aston Research](https://research.aston.ac.uk/en/publications/determinants-of-b2b-e-commerce-adoption-in-saudi-arabian-firms/), [Bedfordshire thesis](https://researchportal.beds.ac.uk/p/en/studenttheses/investigating-e-procurement-barriers-within-six-saudi-arabian-sme-3). The work is old.

### Inferences
- In small presses and converters, the owner or manager is likely to be both decision-maker and buyer, ordering from a phone. Formal procurement roles and desktop use are more likely in larger converters and brand companies. Designers at brands are probably influencers who browse finishes and request samples. The product therefore needs mobile-first ordering for owners and shareable spec and sample pages for designers.

### Gaps
- No device-split or persona data for GCC B2B buyers.

## 6. Barriers to marketplace adoption and what worked (Tradeling, Sary, Udaan, IndiaMART)

### Takeaway
Across cases, two things decided outcomes: whether the platform could carry relationship-based trust and credit, and whether it moved real transactions rather than only generating leads. Sary scaled in KSA by aggregating fragmented small buyers (grocers, cafés, restaurants). Udaan suffered from bad debts on credit to retailers. IndiaMART's transactional arm, Tolexo, failed partly for lack of credit.

### Cited Findings
- Sary (KSA):
  - Serves grocery stores, restaurants, cafés, hotels and canteens.
  - Facilitated SAR 1B (about $267M) of purchases for 30,000+ SMEs in two years (2021) — [Arab News](https://arab.news/5kud4).
  - Raised a $75M round led by PIF-owned Sanabil, for $112M total — [The National](https://thenational-the-national-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/business/start-ups/2021/12/19/saudi-digital-marketplace-sary-raises-75m-in-funding-round-led-by-pif-backed-sanabil).
  - Planned embedded finance to ease cash flow, and coverage notes that small businesses still find credit hard to access — [MENAbytes](https://www.menabytes.com/sary-series-b/).
- Tradeling (UAE):
  - Credit-led adoption features: 30, 60 and 90-day terms, the AECB tie-up, the Invoice Bazaar partnership, and Mastercard and Amazon Payment Services partnerships (2020–2023) — [ibsintelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/al-etihad-credit-bureau-and-tradeling-team-up-to-offer-uae-businesses-better-credit-facilities), [Zawya](https://zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/how-is-tradeling-shaping-the-future-of-b2b-ecommerce-in-mena-lyys5abb).
  - I found no 2024–2026 data on its traction or strategy.
- Udaan / Indian B2B:
  - Analysts argue platforms tried to convert relationship-based credit into scalable app-based credit — [Vanik blog](https://www.vanik.com/blogs/?p=2575); [Morning Context](https://themorningcontext.com/internet/udaan-and-b2b-e-commerce).
  - One blog says Udaan's lending to small retailers produced bad debts — [IdeaProof](https://ideaproof.io/failure/udaan-struggles). This is a secondary source and is unverified.
- IndiaMART:
  - A lead-generation and classifieds model. Its transactional venture Tolexo shut in 2017 amid dwindling sales; lack of credit is cited as one reason it found no takers.
  - It is now experimenting with SaaS and credit — [Morning Context](https://themorningcontext.com/internet/udaan-and-b2b-e-commerce); [IndiaMART corporate](https://corporate.indiamart.com/indiamart-2/).
- Sabi (Nigeria, as a comparable case): cut about 20% of staff and pivoted away from an FMCG marketplace model (2025) — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202506230369.html).

### Inferences
- Barriers for MaterialHub, all of which are hypotheses:
  - Buyers already have trusted traders who give credit.
  - Prices are negotiated per buyer, so public list prices strain relationships.
  - Buyers and sellers may take the deal off-platform through WhatsApp after first contact. The literature describes this as the lead-gen versus transaction trade-off.
  - Specs are complex (GSM, finish, size, colour).
  - Small traders lack the capacity to keep catalogues and stock current.
- Levers that worked elsewhere:
  - Embedded credit, with underwriting through AECB or a fintech partner.
  - Aggregating fragmented small buyers.
  - Reliable delivery.
  - Making the platform the system of record (invoices, e-invoicing, reorder history), which keeps transactions on-platform.
- The MaterialHub roadmap's RFQ, quantity breaks, samples and business accounts with trade credit line up with these levers. Trade credit looks like the most decisive item for B2B retention.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 performance data for Tradeling or Sary; Sary reportedly expanded into Egypt and Pakistan, but this is unverified.
- No disintermediation or leakage rates for GCC B2B marketplaces.
- No GCC case study for paper and packaging specifically.

## 7. Differences across GCC countries (KSA vs UAE vs Kuwait, etc.)

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. KSA has the best-funded B2B marketplace (Sary, backed by PIF's Sanabil) and a cluster of B2B BNPL fintechs (Bynow, Mala). The UAE has the deepest payment-practice data (Atradius) and a 2027 e-invoicing mandate. No comparable Kuwait, Qatar, Oman or Bahrain buyer-behaviour data surfaced.

### Cited Findings
- KSA B2B fintech and marketplaces: Sary [Arab News](https://arab.news/5kud4); Bynow [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/fintech/merak-capital-invests-12mln-in-bynow-to-foster-regional-expansion-rje6wapt); Mala [ScenoNoise](https://dev.scenenoise.com/Business/Saudi-B2B-Procure-Now-Pay-Later-Platform-Mala-Secures-7-Million).
- Historical Coface: payment delays in KSA were tied to government payouts to contractors (2017) — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/?p=96791).
- Bahrain also amended its law to decriminalise bounced cheques — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/gcc/bahrain/bahrain-politics-economics/law-amendment-decriminalising-bounced-cheques-explained).

### Inferences
- KSA is the larger demand pool. It probably needs Arabic-first UX, Shariah-compliant credit and ZATCA e-invoicing (Fatoora) integration; the ZATCA point comes from my background knowledge and was not sourced in this session. The UAE is a natural beachhead, with a re-export hub and a multilingual expat trader base. Kuwait and the other smaller markets are best served as cross-border extensions from UAE vendors.

### Gaps
- No country-comparative survey of B2B buyer behaviour in the GCC.
- No Kuwait-, Qatar- or Oman-specific data found.
