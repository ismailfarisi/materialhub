# Surface brief: storefront (apps/web)

Scope: every route under `apps/web/src/app/[locale]`. Mode: **Persuade** for the home page, collection pages and category landings; **Operate** for listings, search, product detail, cart, checkout, account and auth; **Read** for static pages (about, faq, terms, privacy, shipping, returns, press, careers, contact, sell).

Audience: trade buyers first (packaging, print, rigid-box, bookbinding, leather-goods workshops), retail craft shoppers second. See /PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: MaterialHub is the swatch book a trade buyer already keeps on the desk. It refuses the category default: a neutral marketplace grid of photo tiles with a coloured "Shop now" banner.

OWN-WORLD: Board-grey ground, white stock cards, binder-ink type. Seven category stocks (paper blue, oxblood, brass, laminate green, kraft, grosgrain red, tissue violet) used only as whole fields: card tabs, category bands, fan blades. Archivo for display, Archivo condensed caps for mill labels, Geist for UI, Geist Mono for specs. Cutter orange only for the next action. Cut-card corners (6px), no pills.

STORY: A buyer sees the whole materials range as a fanned book, opens the category stock they need, filters by spec, compares swatch cards that show supplier, specs and price per unit, then orders a sample or adds to cart.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): the CMS hero carousel stays as the lead (it is CMS-owned); below it, the seven category stocks as a row of tall swatch blades, each opening its category. The search field in the header is the primary action for returning buyers.

FORM: Swatch book / materials library (direction 1 of 3 offered; chosen by the owner on 2026-11-02). seed: none (unavailable). The Impeccable launcher was blocked in this environment, so concept-seed did not run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
