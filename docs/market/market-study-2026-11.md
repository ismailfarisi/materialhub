# MaterialHub market study (November 2026)

Desk research from public web sources, gathered 6 November 2026. Market-size figures come from paid analyst reports with different scopes. They disagree, so treat them as direction, not fact. Sources are listed at the end.

## 1. Summary

1. **The core market is large and growing slowly; one niche is growing fast.** UAE packaging is worth about USD 10.8 bn in 2025, growing about 5% a year to about USD 14.75 bn in 2031. Pharmaceutical packaging grows about 16% a year. A single catalogue that covers the whole materials chain is still a viable position, because no one owns it online.
2. **Regulation is creating demand.** The federal single-use plastics ban widened on 1 January 2026 (cups, lids, cutlery, plates, straws, foam containers, and bags under 50 microns of any material). Buyers are switching to paper, moulded fibre and reusable formats, and need to know which products comply.
3. **E-invoicing becomes mandatory for B2B in 2027.** UAE e-invoicing runs on Peppol. Large businesses (AED 50 m+ turnover) go live on 1 January 2027 and everyone else on 1 July 2027, with fines of AED 5,000 a month. Every B2B invoice MaterialHub issues will need buyer and seller TRNs and a structured (UBL / PINT AE) form sent through an Accredited Service Provider. Today MaterialHub only issues PDF receipts.
4. **Trade buyers want self-service, specs, custom pricing and payment terms.** 73% prefer buying online, but only 19% say online buying meets their expectations. 83% rate self-service tools as very important. The most valued features are detailed product information and custom pricing. 94% research payment options before buying.
5. **B2B pay-later is an established market in the UAE.** About USD 1.5 bn in volume in 2025 and about USD 1.97 bn in 2026, growing about 24% a year. Comfi offers 30/60/90-day terms to SMEs; Tabby and Tamara are launching business products. Net terms are now expected, not a premium feature.
6. **The generalists are big; the specialist gap is open.** Tradeling (state-backed, more than 50,000 buyers and 120,000 sellers across 14 categories), Amazon Business and noon Business are horizontal. Specialist packaging suppliers in Dubai mostly run brochure sites with "request a quote" forms and no checkout. Rigid-box makers quote minimum orders of 25 to 100 units at AED 25 to 60 per box. Nobody combines specs, samples, tiered prices and checkout.
7. **Shoppers expect speed and mobile.** Smartphones carry about 50% to 79% of UAE e-commerce, depending on the source. 45% of MENA shoppers say slow delivery frustrates them most, and 69% want free next-day delivery. Digital wallets take about 44% of online payment share.

## 4. What this means for the product

| Finding | Product response | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers want custom pricing and detailed specs | Trade pricing: unit of measure, minimum order, quantity-break tiers, pack multiples | P0 |
| Specialists sell through quote forms; buyers test material first | Sample SKUs with capped quantity and discounted shipping; requests for quotation to up to three suppliers | P0 |
| E-invoicing is mandatory from 2027; buyers check invoice options | Business accounts with TRN and trade licence; VAT tax invoices with both TRNs; a Peppol-ready invoice data model and UBL export; vendor TRNs | P0 (deadline-driven) |
| B2B pay-later is expected; 94% check payment options | Net terms for approved business accounts, with an admin-set credit limit. Partner pay-later (Comfi, Tabby Business) later | P1 |
| Plastic-ban substitution | Compliance attributes and filters ("plastic-ban ready", recyclable, compostable, plastic-free, FSC), a food-service paper packaging category, and a landing collection | P1 |
| Delivery speed frustrates shoppers | Delivery promise ("Get it by Tue, 12 Nov"), with dispatch cutoff and handling time per warehouse; same-day and next-day badges | P1 |
| Mobile-heavy traffic; 85% of buyers plan more mobile buying | Every workstream is designed mobile-first (already a Swatch Book rule) | Standing |

## 5. Risks and open questions

- Market-size figures conflict across analysts; none are audited.
- E-invoicing: MaterialHub must choose an Accredited Service Provider before the July 2027 SME deadline. Whether the marketplace or each vendor is the issuer of each sub-order's invoice is a legal question to confirm with a tax adviser.
- Net terms carry credit risk. Start with a small admin-approved pilot, or partner with a regulated provider.
- Tradeling may add vertical features. MaterialHub's advantage is depth in specs, samples and swatches.
